Vehicle Maintenance Guide

Regular vehicle maintenance helps keep a vehicle reliable and safe.

Engine oil should be checked according to the vehicle manufacturer's
recommended maintenance schedule.

Tyres should be inspected regularly for visible damage, unusual wear,
and appropriate inflation pressure.

Brake components should be inspected during scheduled servicing.

The recommended service interval depends on the vehicle model,
manufacturer recommendations, mileage, usage conditions, and service
schedule.

Always follow the vehicle manufacturer's official maintenance
instructions for exact service intervals and specifications.
